# FIFA World Cup 2026 – Objective 2: Linear Regression (Assessment 3)

Two linear regression models built from pre-match information only:

| Task | Predicts | Rows | Explanatory variables |
|------|----------|------|-----------------------|
| 2.1  | Goal difference of a match (Team A - Team B) | 104 | 8 (rank gap, host, same confederation, stage, titles, rest, points, confederation strength) |
| 2.2  | Goals scored by one team in one match        | 208 | 8 (rank advantage, host, same confederation, stage, own scoring form, opponent conceding form, team/opponent is European) |

The brief allows at most 4 shared variables: we share exactly 4 (ranking gap, host, same confederation, stage).

## Data notes
- Results: 104 matches of the 2026 World Cup. FIFA ranks: MLSsoccer.com, 11 June 2026. Prior titles, hosts and confederations are fixed facts.
- Squad age and squad size from FBref were NOT used: they are calculated over the whole tournament, so they would leak the match outcomes.
- Form and points use only earlier matches, with a prior of 1.3 goals so first matches have a value. Rest days are capped at 7.

## Method in one paragraph
OLS with the 8 variables is fitted on all rows (robust standard errors) to read the coefficients. Then 7 models are compared with
5-fold cross-validation repeated 30 times (both rows of a match stay in the same fold for Task 2.2).
